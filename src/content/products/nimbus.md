## What it does

An accessibility-focused virtual controller for Windows. Move your mouse, get virtual joystick output via vJoy. Designed for adaptive gaming, flight sims, racing, and UAV/rover control workflows.

## Features

- **Mouse-to-joystick mapping**: fine-grained sensitivity curves, deadzones, axis inversion.
- **Multiple control schemes**: flight sim, racing, FPS, custom profile per game.
- **vJoy backend**: works with any game or app that reads a standard joystick.
- **Free and open source**: MIT-licensed, community-driven.

## Built for

Gamers with motor disabilities who can't hold a physical controller, flight-sim and racing enthusiasts who want unique control schemes, and operators who need precise stick input from a mouse.
