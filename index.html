How I build

Principles

By and for

OKStudio is disability-led. It is founded and run by a disabled engineer, and the tools are shaped by the people who depend on them.

Nothing about us without us

I build with disabled people from the start. Lived experience shapes what gets made and how; it is never an afterthought at the end.

Open source

The software is open for the community to use, inspect, fork, and improve. No black boxes around tools people rely on.

Accessible and private by design

Screen-reader-first, keyboard-only, and built to WCAG 2.2 AA, with your data kept on your device wherever it can be.

Flagship

Coverage Compass

A free, open-source tool that helps disabled Coloradans keep their Medicaid under Colorado's 2027 work-reporting rules. It reads and explains your Medicaid letters right on your device (live demo), with document form-filling in preview. Everything runs in your browser: no server, no account, and your documents never leave your device. Available in English and Spanish, built to WCAG 2.2 AA, in partnership with the Colorado Cross-Disability Coalition.

About

I'm Owen Kent, a wheelchair user and disabled software engineer, and I run OKStudio, a disability-led software studio. I build accessibility-first tools by and for people with disabilities.

My work follows one rule: nothing about us without us. I build with disabled people from the first idea to the shipped release, so the tools reflect how we actually live and work, not how someone assumes we do. Everything is open source, screen-reader-first, keyboard-only, and built to WCAG 2.2 AA, with privacy handled by keeping your data on your device wherever possible.

Outside the studio I'm also an FAA-certified Remote Pilot and a licensed ham radio operator, a couple of maker credentials that keep me curious about how hardware, software, and people connect.

Working on an accessibility project, a community tool, or a partnership? Whether you're a disability organization, a fellow builder, or someone who hit a wall with software that wasn't made for you, I'd like to hear about it.
