About OKStudio

OKStudio is founded by Owen Kent, a builder working at the intersection of accessibility, engineering, and media. The studio focuses on assistive technology, accessibility-first software, and interaction design—building tools that expand what people can do with computers, games, and creative software.

From adaptive input systems to voice-driven automation and musical interfaces, OKStudio blends clean engineering, modern UX, and practical accessibility into products that are fast, usable, and genuinely empowering.
